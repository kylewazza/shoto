Every New Year's Eve ends the same way. At midnight, forty phones go up to film the countdown, and the next day nobody can find a single photo with people in it. You have twelve shaky videos of fireworks and one blurry selfie from the kitchen.

Shoto fixes that by giving every guest a disposable camera on their own phone, with a limited number of shots and no way of looking back at them. You see everything on New Year's Day.

## How it works at a New Year's Eve party

1. **Set up your event.** Choose a package, add the date and pick when the photos are revealed. You get a QR code by email straight away.
2. **Put the QR code out.** By the drinks, on the fridge, on a screen, or in the group chat before people arrive.
3. **Guests scan and shoot.** The camera opens in their phone's browser. There's nothing to download and nobody needs an account.
4. **Open the gallery in the new year.** At the reveal time you chose, every photo from the night is waiting in your private gallery, with our film look and as clean originals.

## Why it works so well on New Year's Eve

**People save shots for midnight.** With a limited number of shots, guests stop snapping everything and start choosing. Tell everyone to keep a few back for the countdown and you'll get midnight from every corner of the room, not just one angle.

**Nobody spends the night checking their photos.** Guests can't see what they've taken, so phones go back in pockets and people get on with the party.

**The reveal is a good way to start the year.** Instead of scrolling through other people's stories on New Year's Day, you open a gallery of your own night, taken by the people who were there.

**It works for any kind of night.** A house party, a dinner with friends, a hired room above a pub or a big night at a venue. If you're running a larger New Year's Eve event, have a look at [Shoto Events](/events).

## Tips for the night

- **Tell people about the midnight shots.** A message in the group chat on the day ("save some shots for midnight") makes a real difference.
- **Put the QR code where people gather.** The drinks table and the kitchen beat the hallway.
- **Share your Wi-Fi.** Guests need mobile data or Wi-Fi to send their shots, so if signal is patchy where you are, put the Wi-Fi password next to the QR code.
- **Pick a kind reveal time.** 10am on New Year's Day is ambitious for most people. Early afternoon on the 1st gives everyone time to wake up first.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do guests need to download an app?**
No. They scan the QR code and the camera opens in their phone's browser.

**Can guests see their photos on the night?**
No. Nobody sees anything until the reveal, and then only you see the gallery.

**Can I choose when the photos are revealed?**
Yes. You pick the date and time when you set up the event, so New Year's Day afternoon is fine.

**Does it work on iPhone and Android?**
Yes. It runs in Safari on iPhone and Chrome on Android, so there's nothing to install.

**How long do I have to download the photos?**
Photos are kept for 90 days after your reveal, and you can download everything in one go.
